Reports
  • Rule: hover lifts (3px hard shadow), pressed sinks (inset), disabled goes dashed, read-only loses its edge — engaged is the one licensed exception: green, square, 4px left edge.1 extra colour
  • Borderless roles get their own channel: links, nav items and tertiary buttons take a fill step plus an inset edge marker, since a shadow needs a box to sit on.
  • Works anywhere — dark mode, print, colour-blind users, and inside colourful data views where a blue hover would collide.
  • Cost: a 1.5px→3px border is a quiet signal; needs generous hit areas and consistent spacing to read at all.
